## Key words explained (read this first)

- **Tuition:** the money a university charges for classes each year.
- **Living costs:** money for rent, food, transport and phone.
- **Stipend / allowance:** money a scholarship pays you every month to live on.
- **Full scholarship:** pays tuition *and* gives money for living. A "tuition waiver" only removes tuition.
- **QS / THE rankings:** two big lists of the world's best universities. **QS** = Quacquarelli Symonds. **THE** = Times Higher Education. "QS Computer Science ranking" is a separate QS list for CS only.
- **SAT:** an American test of Math and English (score 400–1600).
- **AS Level / A Level:** Cambridge exams. AS = the first half (one year). A Level = the full course (two years).
- **Predicted grades:** the grades your teachers expect you to get. Universities use them before your real results come out.
- **Conditional offer:** "You are admitted *if* you get these grades." **Unconditional offer:** you are admitted with no more conditions.
- **CSCA:** China Scholastic Competency Assessment, a new Chinese entrance test for foreign students.
- **Studienkolleg:** a one-year preparatory college in Germany for students whose school certificate is not enough to enter a German university.
- **Work obligation:** a promise to work in Uzbekistan for some years after your studies, in return for the scholarship.
- **Holistic admission:** the university looks at everything (grades, essays, projects, letters), not only test scores.
- **Numerus fixus:** a program with a fixed, limited number of places.

## 2. El-Yurt Umidi Foundation (EYUF): the real rules

**Main legal source:** Presidential Decree PF-77 of 5 May 2025 (published on lex.uz, amended 29 October 2025). The foundation's sites are eyuf.uz, admission.eyuf.uz and the application platform el-yurt.uz.

**Does it fund bachelor's study? Yes.**
- The 2026 first open competition had **500 places**: 167 for bachelor's, 232 for master's, 38 for PhD, plus 63 extra places tied to Presidential decisions (ApplyBoard's 2026/27 guide gives the same numbers).
- Applications ran **3–24 April 2026**, online at el-yurt.uz. Tests were held on **25–26 April 2026**.
- The fund has said it runs competitions **at least twice a year** (Asianlite, 2023), but the dates move: TDTU's announcement shows the 2025 first competition opened on 3 June 2025 with 450 places (130 bachelor's, 220 master's, 100 doctoral). In 2025 the window was 3–16 June. In 2023 the second competition ran 10 October – 10 November.
- **Age:** bachelor's applicants must be **30 or younger**.
- **Language:** at least **IELTS 6.0** (or equal) for bachelor's. Your 7.0–7.5 is enough.
- **Priority fields:** STEM and IT get priority. Computer Science fits well.
- **Program length funded:** 3–4 years for a bachelor's.

**Your belief about "top 300": partly right, with one correction.**
- For **bachelor's**, the decree uses the **QS** world ranking. (THE is used for master's and PhD.)
- The fund's platform also has a **list of accepted universities** and a list of **priority specialties**. Check that your university and program are on these lists.
- The three "no competition" routes in the decree:
  1. **QS top 100 + full tuition grant** → the fund gives you its scholarship without a competition.
  2. **QS top 300 + full tuition grant** → the fund pays **international travel and housing** without a competition.
  3. **QS top 20 + at least 50% tuition grant + graduate of a Presidential, creative or specialized school** → the fund gives its scholarship without a competition. **This route is made for you.**
- In the normal **open competition**, you get selected by a **test** (organized by the Knowledge and Skills Assessment Agency) and an **interview**. The test checks thinking, analysis, creativity and psychological stability.

**Documents for bachelor's applicants (2026 competition):**
- An application letter to the fund's Executive Director (the platform has a sample).
- An **acceptance letter** from the foreign university.
- A copy of your school certificate (attestat) or diploma.
- An international language certificate (IELTS etc.).
- A **motivation essay**.
- A copy of your work record book (only if you have one).

**Can you apply with a conditional offer?**
- **Past rules said yes:** the 2022 rules and the 2023 second competition accepted a "Letter of conditional acceptance".
- **The 2026 list only says "Letter of acceptance".** It does not clearly say "conditional".
- **What this means for you:** most A Level students only have a *conditional* offer in spring 2028. Before you rely on Plan B, ask the fund directly (Telegram bot @eyufadmissionbot, phone +998 71 203 14 41) whether a conditional offer is accepted in that year's competition.

**What the fund pays:**
- Full tuition.
- Visa costs.
- A round trip between Uzbekistan and your university, up to once a year.
- Living, food, medical insurance and local transport.
- Books, and access to journals and databases.
- The fund's Board of Trustees sets a **maximum amount** per scholar. Very expensive programs may go over this cap.

**Who cannot use the open competition:**
- People whose studies are already **fully paid** by a foreign grant (examples named: Chevening, Fulbright, Erasmus Mundus, DAAD, KOICA, JICE).
- So if MEXT or GKS pays for everything, you do not need El-Yurt Umidi, and you cannot get both.

**Work obligation after graduation:**
- After a **bachelor's: 5 years** of continuous, full-time work in Uzbekistan. (Master's: 3 years. PhD: 5 years.)
- You usually work for a state body or organization that signs a contract with you and the fund.
- With the Board's approval, you may serve your years in education, research, health care, or certain important private or public-private companies.
- You may do an internship abroad after graduating, but that time **does not count** toward the 5 years.
- **Penalty:** if you break the contract, **you and your guarantors must pay back double** all the money spent on you.

### Singapore: NUS and NTU
- **A Levels:** NUS asks for "a good pass in at least **four** A Level subjects". AS Levels do **not** count as final grades. You have three (CS, Physics, Maths), so **add a fourth**. Further Mathematics is best.
- **SAT:** NUS says SAT, ACT or AP is **required** if your A Level exams finish after the application deadline. That will be your case for 2028 entry. A strong SAT (about 1450–1500+) is very important here.
- **English test:** not required for A Level students. You may still send your IELTS.
- **Dates:** for 2027 entry, the international window was 3 December 2025 – 23 February 2026 (a year earlier). Expect about **December 2027 – February 2028** for 2028 entry. Scholarship forms come after the admission application.
- **Scholarships:** for international students NUS lists the ASEAN Undergraduate Scholarship (not for you), the **Science & Technology Undergraduate Scholarship**, the NUS International Undergraduate Scholarship, and the ABS–Goh Keng Swee Scholarship. ### Hong Kong: HKU and HKUST
