## Watch out: two different olympiads

In 2026 there were two international school cybersecurity olympiads in the same week:

| | **ICO** – International Cybersecurity Olympiads | **ICOA** – International Cyber Olympiad in AI |
|---|---|---|
| Website | [ico-official.net](https://ico-official.net/) | [icoa2026.au](https://icoa2026.au/index.html) |
| Started | 2025, Singapore (NUS), founded by Prof. Tan Sun Teck | 2026 (first edition) |
| 2026 | Yasmine Hammamet, Tunisia, 27 June – 2 July 2026 ([HKUST](https://cse.hkust.edu.hk/News/ICO2026/), [ico2026.tn](https://ico2026.tn)) | Sydney, Australia, 27 June – 2 July 2026 ([KPI](https://icd.kpi.ua/?p=32266)) |
| Format | Individual jeopardy CTF, 2 days × 7 hours | AI4CTF ("AI as your teammate") + embodied-AI security round, team and individual medals |

**Which one does Uzbekistan's selection feed?** The national portal [ico26.uz](https://ico26.uz) calls itself the selection for "ICO 2026 in Sydney, Australia" and its national final had an "ICOA" round. [Yuz.uz](https://yuz.uz/en/news/v-uzbekistane-startoval-otbor-na-mejdunarodnuyu-olimpiadu-po-kiberbezopasnosti-ico-2026) also says ICO 2026 is in Australia. So the Uzbek selection seems to send its team to the **Sydney (ICOA)** event, not Tunisia. **Unknown — ask me** (or ask the ICO Uzbekistan Selection Committee through the contact form on ico26.uz) which olympiad the 2027 selection is for.

## Uzbekistan national selection (2026 cycle)

Source: [Yuz.uz, 22 April 2026](https://yuz.uz/en/news/v-uzbekistane-startoval-otbor-na-mejdunarodnuyu-olimpiadu-po-kiberbezopasnosti-ico-2026), [ico26.uz](https://ico26.uz).

- Who can take part: students in **grades 7–11**. Free of charge.
- Registration: at ico26.uz, deadline **30 April 2026**. First stage on **3 May 2026**.
- Stages (Yuz.uz): 1 online stage, then 2 offline stages. The portal shows 4 selection rounds → top 50 → top 15 → **5-person national team**.
- National final 2026: a "Terminal Round (ICOA)" and a "CTFd Round", 56 finalists.
- Organizer: "ICO Uzbekistan Selection Committee" (named on the portal footer). Which ministry or agency runs it: **unknown — ask me**.
- **2027 selection dates: unknown — ask me.** If it follows 2026, registration would be around April 2027. Watch ico26.uz (or a new ico27.uz) and Telegram channels from about March 2027.

## ICO (ico-official.net) rules, 2026 edition

Source: [ICO 2026 Rules (PDF)](https://ico-official.net/ICO%202026%20-%20Rules.pdf).

- Eligibility: enrolled at a secondary school during Sep–Dec of the year before; **not older than 20 on 1 July** of the olympiad year; no university students.
- Team: 1–4 contestants per country + leader (+ optional deputy).
- Format: **individual jeopardy-style CTF**, **two 7-hour days**, 3 tasks per day, 100 points each, split into ~4–6 subtasks. Flags look like `ICO{...}`. Ties broken by time of last score.
- **Topics (syllabus):**
  - Binary exploitation (x86-64 only)
  - Cryptography
  - Digital forensics
  - Reverse engineering
  - Web security
  - Miscellaneous: mobile, network, algorithmic/programming, AI, cloud, radio
- Tools: your own laptop, open-source/free software. Internet through a logged VPN; search engines and online decompilers allowed. **General AI use is banned**, except an in-house chat (gpt-5-mini, 100k tokens per day).
- Medals need skills across many topics, not just one.
- Past tasks for practice: [archive.ico-official.net](https://archive.ico-official.net).
